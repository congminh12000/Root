# Faction panels

`marquise-panel.webp`: the bottom-left badge of your own faction when you play the Marquise de Cat.
Original artwork made by the project owner (self-drawn or generated), supplied on 2026-11-05 and approved for the
repository. It is not official Leder Games art. Resized from 1555 × 1012 to 780 × 508 and re-encoded as WebP
(quality 0.82, alpha kept) for load speed.

The painting holds a laurel for the score (bottom left) and three icons on the right: warriors in supply, wood in
supply, cards in hand. `ui/faction-art.ts` lists that order; the HUD writes the numbers over the painting.
